Common questions

FAQ

Is the instant quote my final price?

No. It's a ballpark based on typical homes your size. Your exact written price comes after our free on-site inspection, where we measure the roof and check the decking, flashing and ventilation.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. The on-site estimate is free with no obligation, and you'll get a written price you can take your time with.

Will insurance cover my roof?

If your roof has storm damage from hail or wind, your policy may cover it, and you'd typically pay your deductible. We document the damage with photos and can meet with your adjuster.

How long does a replacement take?

Most homes are done in one to two days, depending on size, weather and material. We clean up and magnet-sweep for nails before we leave.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Every job includes our workmanship warranty, plus the manufacturer's warranty on materials. We'll go over the details with your written estimate.
